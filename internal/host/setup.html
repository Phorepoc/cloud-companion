Cloud setup

Running from an SD card

For one person, a good card is fine: use a high-endurance card (sold for dash cams and security cameras), which is made for constant writes. The board keeps its logs and swap in memory to spare it, and backups (in Cloud Companion's settings) keep your data safe if the card fails. A USB SSD (a Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 starts from USB on its own) is faster and lasts longer.

Welcome

Internet

The board needs the internet to download Cloud Companion. Plug in a network cable, or pick your Wi-Fi.

Joining

The board is leaving its own "Cloud setup" Wi-Fi, so this page will stop answering. Put your phone or computer back on and open http://cloud-companion.local to carry on.

If the board cannot join, "Cloud setup" comes back in a minute with the reason.

New or restored?

Set up a new Cloud Companion, or bring one back from its backup: everything (chats, mail, files, features and your devices) returns as it was.
